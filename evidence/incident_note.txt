INCIDENT NOTE
=============
Northstar Systems reported suspicious access to finance-related files.

IMPORTANT CONTEXT
-----------------
- Failed login attempts alone do NOT prove compromise.
- One external IP in the evidence is a legitimate approved admin VPN address.
- The evidence does NOT prove that any files were successfully exfiltrated.

FAST INVESTIGATION PATH
-----------------------
STEP 1 — Open auth.log.
Find a LOGIN_SUCCESS event that came from an external-looking IP address.

STEP 2 — Check that source IP in vpn_access.csv.
Determine whether it was approved or unapproved.

STEP 3 — Check that username in employee_directory.txt.
Determine whether the account should have been active.

STEP 4 — Return to auth.log.
Look at the events immediately after that successful login and record the files accessed.

STEP 5 — Find one red herring.
Identify something that looks suspicious but does NOT prove a successful compromise.

CONTAINMENT CLUE
----------------
Once you identify an account that should not be active, the first goal is to stop it from being used again.

FINAL FLAG FORMAT
-----------------
Use the suspicious account name and its status:

FLAG{<ACCOUNT>_<STATUS>}

Use uppercase letters.

Example:
If the suspicious account were test_user and its status were ACTIVE:
FLAG{TEST_USER_ACTIVE}
